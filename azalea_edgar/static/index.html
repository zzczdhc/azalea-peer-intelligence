How to read these results

A name needs a direct competition statement or a business list explicitly introduced as a source of competition. A Competition heading alone is insufficient. The extractor keeps the statement and its introduction so you can verify the relation.

SEC directory matches check company-name spelling. They do not verify the competitive relationship. Brands, subsidiaries, historical names and ambiguous identities need review. An unmatched name does not establish listing status.

These rules support selected disclosure formats. This is a candidate set for review; coverage, business similarity and financial comparability still need evaluation.
