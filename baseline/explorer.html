How classic TNIC represents a company

The classic TNIC-3 used here starts with 10-K product / business descriptions, retains selected nouns and proper nouns, and removes common words and geographic terms. Each retained word is represented as 1 if present and 0 otherwise. The vector is normalized, then compared with cosine similarity. A calibrated threshold defines the peer set.

This is a binary bag-of-words representation, not a trained semantic embedding model. TF-IDF changes the word weights; modern embeddings learn vector representations. Newer embedding-based TNIC releases are separate from the classic TNIC-3 file loaded here. Hoberg & Phillips (2016).

Feature definitions and coverage

Size = natural log of assets; net margin = net income / revenue; growth = current / prior annual revenue − 1 using the same revenue tag; leverage proxy = total liabilities / assets (not debt / assets); capital intensity = net PPE / assets. Net PPE includes the reported combined finance-lease ROU asset amount when the plain net PPE tag is unavailable. USD, consolidated US-GAAP annual facts only.

How this text comparison is computed

Latest non-amended 10-K filed during the selected calendar year, matched by exact accession number to Item 1 Business text. The descriptive-year corpus supplies IDF weights. Tokens use lowercase letter unigrams, a fixed English / filing stop list, sublinear term frequency, smoothed IDF and L2 normalization. Very short texts and overly common / unique terms are excluded. This is not a TNIC replication or a prediction backtest.

Direction 4 · Peer-relative metrics

Compute a company metric minus its peers' mean or median, then compare raw metrics, SIC-relative metrics, TNIC-relative metrics and our peer-relative metrics on a preselected future outcome. This is a separate quantitative research test; the peer explorer does not yet report a predictive win.

From an existing customer to a comparable prospect

Business scenario: company A uses an S&P product. Find company B with comparable business activities and a supported reason to investigate a similar need. This workspace contains no customer purchase records or product-fit labels.
